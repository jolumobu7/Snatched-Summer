Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE YOU PUBLISH]

Snatched by Summer ("the app") is made by Jesufifunmi Olugbenga ("we", "us"). This policy covers the iPhone app, the Android app, and the web version at this site.

The short version

We don't collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores, and where

To work, the app saves the following only on your device (in the app's local storage, or your browser's storage for the web version):

  • Your setup answers: goals, training days, experience level, sport styles, and optional sculpting choices.
  • Optional body details you choose to enter: first name, age, height, weight and measurements.
  • Workout logs, check-ins (energy, mood, tags), water tracking and progress.
  • Optional cycle data: period start dates and cycle length, if you turn on cycle sync.
  • Your settings: theme, units and reminder times.

This information is not sent to us or to anyone else. We have no servers that receive it and no account system.

What we collect

Nothing. The app contains no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no ad tracking and no crash reporting.

Cycle and health information

Cycle and health-related data you enter never leaves your device. We can't see it, so we can't share it, sell it, or hand it over to anyone. If a future version offers cloud sync, it will be off by default, and we will explain exactly what changes before you could turn it on. This policy will be updated first.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally by your phone's operating system. No push-notification server is involved.

Backups

If you use "Save a backup file", the app creates a file and hands it to your device's share sheet. Where that file goes (Files, iCloud Drive, Google Drive, email, etc.) is your choice, and that service's own privacy policy applies to it.

Links to other sites

The shop links to Amazon and to supplement-certification websites. When you open them, you leave the app, and those sites' privacy policies apply. As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon may use cookies to attribute a purchase to our link; we don't receive any personal information from Amazon about you.

The web version loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address the way any website request does.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete all my data in the app. This removes everything from your device. Deleting the app also removes its data. For the web version, clearing this site's data in your browser removes it.

Children

The app is for adults 18 and over. It is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above. Any change that involves data leaving your device will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
