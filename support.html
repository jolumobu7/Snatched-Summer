Common questions

How does the app decide when to add weight?

When you finish every set of a move at the target reps, the next session adds a little weight (about 5 lb for lower body, 2.5 lb for upper body). Mark a move "Easy" and it jumps a bit more; "Too hard" backs it off. Missed reps keep the weight the same.

A move hurts or the machine is taken. What do I do?

Tap Swap on that exercise. Choose "It hurts" and the app keeps that move out of your plan until you bring it back in Settings. Sharp or lasting pain? Stop and see a doctor or physio.

I missed a workout.

On a rest day, the Today screen offers to let you do the missed session. You can also move today's session to another open day this week with "Can't today? Move it".

How do I change my reminder times?

Settings → Reminders. Set one time for all training days or a different time per day, plus optional check-in and rest-day reminders.

I'm getting a new phone. Will I lose everything?

Your data lives only on your phone, so save a backup first: Settings → Backup → Save a backup file. On your new phone, use Restore from a backup.

Is the cycle feature birth control or medical advice?

No. Cycle sync (beta) is a training tool. Its estimates are rough and should never be used for contraception or medical decisions.

Does the web version sync with my phone?

No. There are no accounts, so the web version saves to your browser only. Use backup and restore to move data between them.

Where does the workout advice come from? Can I challenge it?

Every rule links to published research on the science page and in the app (look for "Why this?"). Each claim shows how strong the evidence is and whether women were studied. Tap "Question this" to dispute it by email or publicly on GitHub. We review every dispute and log the decision.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all my data. It's removed from your device immediately. We never had a copy.
