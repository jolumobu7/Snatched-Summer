Honest science

Built for women's bodies, without the hype

A lot of "science-based" advice for women is oversimplified. We keep what holds up and skip what doesn't.

You need to change workouts every cycle phase.

Bodies differ. Our cycle sync (beta) learns how you feel from quick check-ins, then suggests changes. You decide.

Lifting heavy makes you bulky.

Heavy, progressive lifting is how you get strong and shapely. Visible muscle takes years.

You can burn fat from one spot.

You can't. You can choose where to build muscle, and that's what sculpting is.

Hip dips mean something's wrong.

They're mostly bone structure. Side-glute work softens the look. Nobody needs to erase them.
